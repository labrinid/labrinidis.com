My personal website has finally gotten a much-needed refresh, migrating from
the University of Pittsburgh infrastructure to an independent home at
[labrinidis.com](https://labrinidis.com).

The new site is built with [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio),
a beautiful Jekyll template designed for academics, hosted on
[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), and powered by
[Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/). The migration and design process was assisted
by [Claude](https://claude.ai), which dramatically accelerated my timeline,
by guiding me through the site configuration, migrating data from multiple
sources, and advising on design decisions.

More content will be added over time, including historical news items going
back to 2002. If you find anything broken or missing, feel free to
[let me know](/contact/).
