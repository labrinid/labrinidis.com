Our paper [Big Data and Its Technical Challenges](https://doi.org/10.1145/2611567)
has surpassed 25,000 downloads from the ACM Digital Library!

*H. V. Jagadish, Johannes Gehrke, Alexandros Labrinidis, Yannis Papakonstantinou,
Jignesh M. Patel, Raghu Ramakrishnan, and Cyrus Shahabi. 2014. Big data and its
technical challenges. Commun. ACM 57, 7 (July 2014), 86–94.*

- According to the [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2611567),
  the paper has received **620 citations** and **25,126 downloads**.
- According to [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=UR4eDW4AAAAJ&hl=en),
  the paper has received **1,254 citations**.
- According to [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/author/Alexandros-Labrinidis/1716685),
  the paper has received **906 citations**.
