This course covers practical predictive modeling techniques with a focus on
real-world applications. Topics include:

- Linear Regression
- Data Clustering
- Decision Trees
- Model Evaluation
- Cross-validation and Overfitting
- Feature Selection and Engineering

The course is part of Pitt's online [Master's of Data Science](https://www.sci.pitt.edu/MDS)
program, delivered via Coursera.
