*(Please read before contacting me)*

---

**Q1: I am a University of Pittsburgh student. Can I join your research group?**

Yes! If you are a graduate or undergraduate student in the [Department of
Computer Science](https://www.cs.pitt.edu/) at the University of Pittsburgh
and are interested in data management, please contact me to discuss further.
We are always looking for smart and motivated students to join the
[ADMT Lab](https://db.cs.pitt.edu/group).

---

**Q2: I am not yet a student at the University of Pittsburgh. Can I join your research group?**

To join the lab you would first need to apply and be admitted to the
[Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.pitt.edu/future-students/graduate)
at the University of Pittsburgh. I have **no control** over Graduate Admissions
— they are handled by a committee. Once admissions decisions are made in Spring,
I would be happy to discuss the possibility of joining the ADMT Lab.

---

**Q3: Can I visit your research group?**

If you are a graduate student at a university abroad and will be funded to
visit and work under my supervision for a brief period, please **have your
advisor contact me first** to discuss the possibility. I will not accept such
requests otherwise.

---

**Q4: Do you give out summer internships?**

Depending on research funding, our group typically involves undergraduate
students from the Computer Science Department as research assistants throughout
the year. However, you must **already be a University of Pittsburgh student**.
We cannot offer internships to students from other universities.

---

**Q5: Do you have any staff, postdoc, or visiting scholar positions?**

Not at this point. Check the [news](/news/) page for any future announcements.

---

**Q6: Can we connect on LinkedIn?**

If you are a student in one of my courses, I would love to connect with you
on LinkedIn — please send me a connection request after the term is over.
I always enjoy keeping in touch with past students and seeing the great things
they accomplish.
