ADMT Lab Research Internships (Spring 2023)

Created on December 21, 2022

2022   ·   students

The Advanced Data Management Technologies Laboratory has multiple undergraduate student researcher positions for the Spring 2023 term. We are seeking highly-motivated students interested in doing research as part of a team and excited to work on projects positively impacting the community. Students will work on various projects under the supervision of Dr. Alex Labrinidis. Representative projects include a spatial data dashboard integrating multiple databases and public information display design (like the ones we have for the PittSmartLiving project).

Required Qualifications:

  • Full web-stack development experience (with Python) — ideally have passed CS 1520 and CS 1555

Optional Qualifications:

  • UI design experience
  • DevOps experience
  • Data science / machine learning background

Types of positions:

  • (a) Paid internship (tentatively $15/hour)
  • (b) CS 1950 credit (directed research capstone)
  • (c) CS 1951 credit (directed research)
  • (d) Just for fun

Application instructions:

To apply, please email the following to Dr. Alex Labrinidis (labrinid@cs.pitt.edu) with subject line “ADMT Lab internship”:

  1. A paragraph about what you are looking for in a research internship
  2. Your resume
  3. Your unofficial Pitt transcript (with Fall 2022 grades)
  4. What type(s) of position you are interested in (a–d)
  5. How many hours per week you can devote to this project

Note: This internship posting is from Spring 2023 and is no longer active.
